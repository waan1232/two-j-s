About Us

Your Neighborhood
Party Headquarters

Located at the convenient intersection of the Beltline and Plainfield in Northview, Two J's is a family-run small business dedicated to serving our community with friendly faces, fair prices, and a curated selection you'll love coming back to.

Whether you're picking up a six-pack after work, building your own mix-and-match, or stocking up for a celebration, we're here to help. We also carry snacks, essentials, and travel items to make your stop easy.
